# Pizza Place Sales Analysis

This project analyzes a year’s worth of sales data from a fictional pizza restaurant using Python and Pandas. The dataset includes customer orders, order details, pizza sizes and prices, as well as pizza categories and ingredients.

The goal of the analysis is to turn raw sales data into useful business insights that can support better menu planning, staffing, inventory management, and promotional decisions.

## Key Questions Answered

* What is the total revenue generated?
* How many pizzas and orders were recorded?
* How many pizza types are available?
* What is the average pizza price?
* What are the busiest hours for sales?
* Which day of the week generates the most revenue?
* What are the top five bestselling pizzas?
* How do sales change across the months?
* Which pizza types are underperforming?

## Key Insights

The pizza place generated over **$817,000** in total revenue from more than **21,000 customer orders**. The busiest sales period occurs around lunchtime, while Friday records the highest sales among all days of the week.

The Classic Deluxe Pizza was identified as the bestselling pizza, while the Brie Carre Pizza had the lowest sales. These findings can help the business prioritize popular menu items, prepare adequately for busy periods, and review low-performing pizzas.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The dataset contains four CSV files:

* `orders.csv`
* `order_details.csv`
* `pizzas.csv`
* `pizza_types.csv`

The datasets were merged into one dataframe before carrying out the analysis.
